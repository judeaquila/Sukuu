Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

LearnSasa ("we", "us") is an online learning platform built for Ghana and Africa. It connects independent course creators with students, handles course checkout in Ghanaian cedis, and pays creators their earnings. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using LearnSasa you agree to this policy.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), and role (student or creator).
  • Profile data — information you choose to add, such as a photo URL or bio.
  • Purchase records — which courses you enrolled in, payment references, amounts, currency, and payment channel. We never see or store your card numbers or MoMo PINs; card and mobile-money details are handled entirely by our payment provider, Paystack.
  • Creator payout data — for creators: Mobile Money numbers or bank account details, the registered account name, and the verification status Paystack returns.
  • Learning & community data — lesson progress you make and posts you publish in a course community.
  • Technical data — standard web logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) used to keep the service secure.

3. How we use your data

  • To run your account, deliver the courses you buy, and keep your learning progress.
  • To process payments and payouts through Paystack, including verifying payout account names before money leaves the platform.
  • To email you transactional updates: receipts, payout status changes, and essential account notices.
  • To detect fraud, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Payments and Paystack

All card and Mobile Money payments are processed by Paystack Payments Limited as our payment partner. When you check out, Paystack processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only the transaction result (reference, amount, status and channel) — never your full card number or MoMo credentials.

5. Who else can see your data

  • Creators can see aggregate student counts for their courses and basic enrollment records (learner name/email, course, progress) needed to teach and support you.
  • Service providers — Paystack (payments) and our hosting/email providers — process data on our instructions.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where we are legally required to disclose.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is open. Financial records (purchases, payouts and their snapshots) are retained for the period required by Ghanaian tax and accounting rules, even after an account is closed. You can close your account by emailing us.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to our processing, at any time by contacting hello@learnsasa.com. We respond within a reasonable period. This policy is prepared with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind.

8. Cookies

LearnSasa uses only the cookies it needs to operate: your login session, security tokens (CSRF), and messages shown between pages. We do not run advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

9. Children

LearnSasa is not intended for children under 13. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we will close it.

10. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, and payment details are handled exclusively by Paystack. Payout account changes are verified against Paystack before transfers are made. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will announce them on the site before they take effect. The date above shows when this policy was last updated.
